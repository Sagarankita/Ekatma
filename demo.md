# EKATMA Sahyadri Bio-Pharma Demo Plan

## Demo goal

Show one connected lifecycle in five minutes:

> EKATMA understands the business, determines what applies, helps the officer review with evidence, and keeps the entrepreneur and government on the same lifecycle until certificate issuance.

## Demo components

- **Story:** one Sahyadri Bio-Pharma project moving from submission to certificate.
- **Entrepreneur view:** business context, personalized requirements, query response, resubmission and outcome.
- **Department view:** queue, machine pre-check, evidence review, query, delta scrutiny, inspection and decision.
- **Live actions:** approve a finding, issue a consolidated query, advance a resubmission, schedule inspection and record approval.
- **Shared handoffs:** show the same application changing state in both portals.
- **Close:** certificate issued, then the source-backed Regulatory Assistant and the EKATMA value statement.

Use the Sahyadri Bio-Pharma demo record throughout:

- Business: **Sahyadri Bio-Pharma Pvt Ltd**
- Project: **Internal demo project — Chakan Industrial Area Phase II**
- Application: **APP-2026-MIDC-00187**
- Query: **QRY-2026-MIDC-00187**
- Inspection: **INS-2026-MIDC-00187**
- Approval: **MIDC/BP/2026/00187**
- Certificate: **CERT-MIDC-2026-00187**

## Before recording

1. Open the Entrepreneur portal and Department portal in separate tabs.
2. Open **Demo Mode** and click **Reset Demo** on either side.
3. Confirm the shared starting state is **Submitted**.
4. Keep the compact **Next** button visible, but use it only for safe stage changes. Use the real page actions when demonstrating a workflow action.
5. Do not open every page. The video should tell one clear story, not present the whole product catalogue.

   - Select Sahyadri Bio-Pharma Pvt Ltd.

   - Say: “The entrepreneur enters the correct business and project first.”

   - Show grouped requirements rather than a flat checklist.

   - Open `APP-2026-MIDC-00187`.

Do not spend time opening every document or compliance screen in this five-minute version.

## USP checklist for the presenter

- **Business DNA → Personalized Regulatory Journey:** one business profile drives relevant approvals, documents, dependencies and compliance.
- **Adaptive Documentation:** only relevant information and documents are requested.
- **Machine Pre-check + Human Evidence/Override:** automated checks assist; officers inspect evidence and decide.
- **Cross-form Consistency + Consolidated Query:** mismatches are found across forms and packaged into one structured query.
- **Delta Re-Scrutiny:** only changed information is reviewed after resubmission.
- **Dependency Graph + Parallel Workflows:** parallel work and true prerequisites are visible.
- **End-to-End Synchronization:** Entrepreneur and Department views reflect one lifecycle through certificate issuance.
- **GR Regulatory Assistant / Source-backed RAG:** regulatory explanations point to the relevant rule, clause and passage without replacing the officer decision.

## What not to show in five minutes

- Do not open every sidebar tab.
- Do not explain every database field or technical provenance attribute.
- Do not present the complete regulatory change database.
- Do not spend time on generic prototype records.
- Do not call the Dependency Graph a simple checklist.
- Do not imply that the Regulatory Assistant makes statutory decisions.
